Questions

What is Acme Money for Agents?

It lets you securely connect your UK bank accounts to your AI app using open banking. You can then ask it about your money, like how much you spent on groceries last month or which subscriptions you're paying for.

How does it work?
  1. Click Get started. Claude opens the "Add custom connector" dialog, already filled in. Click Add, then Connect.
  2. Sign in or create an account, then connect your bank: pick it from the list and approve in your banking app.
  3. Ask about your balances, spending by category, subscriptions… across all your connected banks.
Which AI apps does it work with?

Claude (web, desktop and mobile) and Claude Code today. Any app that supports remote MCP servers with OAuth should work too.

Nothing happens when I click "Get started"

See Claude's guide to custom connectors. On Claude Team and Enterprise plans only an Owner can add connectors. Ask your admin to open this link once; then everyone can click Connect in Customize → Connectors.

Which banks can I connect?

Personal current accounts at UK banks, such as Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, Monzo, Nationwide, NatWest, Revolut, Santander and TSB. Credit cards are not currently supported.

Does my AI app see my bank password?

No. You approve the connection in your banking app. Your login never reaches us or your AI app.

Can it move money?

No. Access is read-only: accounts, balances and transactions.

How long does access last?

Access lasts 90 days, then your bank asks you to approve again.

What happens to my data once my AI app has it?

When you ask a question, only the account data needed is fetched and shared into your conversation. From there it's handled under your AI provider's terms and your settings. AI answers can be wrong and are not financial advice.
